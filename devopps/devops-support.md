## 1. [E-commerce] Checkout and Payment 24/7 Support

Providing 24/7 support for e-commerce checkout and payment systems so incidents are triaged, communicated, and resolved within SLA.
Defining severity (e.g. checkout down = P1), escalation to payment providers and security, and runbooks for rollback and customer communication.
Ensuring revenue-impacting issues are resolved quickly and that status and post-incident summaries are clear for business and compliance.

**Skills:** Azure DevOps (Boards, Pipelines for rollback); Azure Monitor, Application Insights, Azure status; on-call, incident management, severity/escalation; payment systems awareness, PCI/compliance; general DevOps (runbooks, status communication, PIRs)

**Roles and Responsibilities:**

- Run on-call and escalation for checkout and payment systems; triage incidents by severity (e.g. checkout down = P1) and ensure response and resolution within SLA.
- Maintain runbooks for checkout and payment: rollback, customer communication, and escalation to payment providers and security; update after incidents and changes.
- Coordinate with payment providers and security during incidents; ensure revenue-impacting issues are resolved quickly and that root cause is communicated appropriately.
- Publish status updates during outages and post-incident summaries after resolution; ensure business and compliance have clear visibility and that “is it just me?” confusion is minimized.
- Track and report on checkout/payment SLAs, MTTR, and incident trends; drive improvements to reduce recurrence and improve response time.
- Support pipeline and deployment rollback when checkout or payment issues are release-related; work with development and platform teams on fix and prevention.
